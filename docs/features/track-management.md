## Overview

Track Management allows you to organize and evaluate your music collection by rating tracks and marking favorites. These actions sync with MusicBee and help personalize your music experience.

---

## Track Rating

### What is Rating?
Rating is a scoring system that helps you evaluate tracks in your collection. Use ratings to:
- **Identify Favorites**: Give high ratings to tracks you love
- **Create Smart Playlists**: Use ratings as criteria for auto-playlists
- **Track Quality**: Rate cover versions, remixes, or live performances differently
- **DJ Assistance**: Help MusicBee's DJ feature prioritize tracks to play

### How to Rate

1. Open the Now Playing View
2. Look for the **Rating Display** (usually shown as stars ⭐)
3. Click on a star to set your rating:
   - **1 Star** ⭐ - Poor
   - **2 Stars** ⭐⭐ - Below Average
   - **3 Stars** ⭐⭐⭐ - Average
   - **4 Stars** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Good
   - **5 Stars** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Excellent

### Quick Rating
Click on different stars to quickly rate the current track without any confirmation.

## Favorites

### What is Favorite?
Marking a track as a favorite adds it to your "Loved" collection in MusicBee. Use favorites to:
- **Quick Collection**: Quickly build a list of your most-loved songs
- **Smart Playlists**: Use the Loved playlist in MusicBee
- **Recommendations**: Help MusicBee suggest similar music
- **Organization**: Easily filter your library by favorites

### How to Mark as Favorite

1. Locate the **Favorite Button** (❤️) in the Now Playing View
2. Click the heart icon to toggle favorite status
3. **When Favorited**: The heart appears filled or highlighted
4. **When Not Favorited**: The heart appears empty or unfilled

### Managing Favorites

**In MusicBee:**
- View all favorites in the "Loved" smart playlist
- Create auto-playlists based on favorites
- Filter the library to show only favorite tracks

## Syncing & Persistence

### Automatic Sync
- Ratings and favorites are saved immediately to MusicBee
- Changes sync across all MusicBee instances
- No manual saving required

### Data Persistence
- Ratings are stored in your MusicBee database
- Favorites are preserved through library updates
- Metadata syncs with MusicBee's tagging system

## Common Workflows

### Build a Favorites List
1. Start playing music
2. When you hear a song you love, click the ❤️ Favorite button
3. Create a "Loved Tracks" playlist in MusicBee from your favorites

### Rate an Album
1. Play tracks from an album
2. Rate each track as it plays
3. Use ratings to identify your favorite songs from that album

### Find Your Highest Rated Songs
1. Open MusicBee
2. Sort your library by rating
3. Create a smart playlist: "Rating >= 4 stars"

## Troubleshooting

**Can't rate or favorite tracks?**
- Ensure you're connected to MusicBee
- Verify MusicBee is running and has a track loaded
- Try `MusicBee Remote: Refresh` to update the view

**Ratings not saving?**
- Check your MusicBee database connectivity
- Verify you have write permissions to the MusicBee database
- Restart MusicBee and try again

**Favorite button not working?**
- Confirm the track is not corrupted in your library
- Try with a different track
- Check MusicBee logs for errors
