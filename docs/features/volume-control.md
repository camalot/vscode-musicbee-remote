## Overview

Volume Control provides easy access to MusicBee's volume settings without leaving your editor. Adjust playback volume or quickly mute audio when needed.

---

## Features

### Volume Adjustment
Adjust the playback volume from 0% to 100%.

- **Visual Control**: Interactive volume slider in the Now Playing View
- **Smooth Adjustment**: Real-time volume changes as you drag the slider
- **Precise Control**: See the exact percentage while adjusting

### Mute / Unmute
Quickly toggle mute state for the audio output.

- **Button**: 🔇 Mute button in the Now Playing View
- **Toggle**: Click to mute, click again to unmute
- **Visual Feedback**: Button appearance changes when audio is muted

---

## Common Tasks

### Adjusting Volume

1. Locate the volume slider in the Now Playing View
2. Click and drag the slider to adjust volume
3. Volume updates in real-time in MusicBee

### Muting Audio

1. Click the 🔇 Mute button in the Now Playing View
2. To unmute, click the button again
3. **Keyboard Shortcut** (if configured): Press your assigned key

### Setting Specific Volume Level

For precise volume control:
1. Click on the volume slider at a specific position
2. The volume will jump to approximately that percentage
3. Fine-tune by dragging the slider

---

## Volume States

| State | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Muted** | Audio output is silenced |
| **Low Volume** | 1-33% of maximum volume |
| **Medium Volume** | 34-66% of maximum volume |
| **High Volume** | 67-100% of maximum volume |

---

## Tips & Tricks

- **Quick Mute**: Use the mute button to temporarily silence audio during calls or notifications
- **Memory**: Your last volume setting is remembered between sessions
- **Fast Adjustment**: Hold and drag the slider for continuous volume changes
- **Sync**: Volume changes in the extension immediately affect MusicBee playback

---

## Troubleshooting

**Volume slider not responding?**
- Ensure you're connected to MusicBee
- Try clicking the Refresh button
- Check that MusicBee is running

**Volume changes not working?**
- Verify the MusicBee Remote Plugin is enabled
- Check your system audio settings
- Ensure MusicBee has permission to adjust audio

**Mute button not working?**
- Check your connection to MusicBee
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting
- Restart MusicBee if necessary
